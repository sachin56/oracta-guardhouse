Why Oracta Stands
Apart

Every security company makes promises. Here's what backs ours — in detail.

6 Key Reasons

What Makes Us Different

📋
Reason 01

Licensed & Fully Compliant

Every Oracta operation is licensed under the Private Security Services Authority of Sri Lanka. Every officer is individually registered, background-checked, and legally deployed. You carry zero liability exposure — we handle full regulatory compliance on your behalf, including annual audits and renewal documentation.

⚡
Reason 02

Rapid Response Guarantee

Our control room operates 24/7/365. The moment an incident is flagged — by a guard, client call, or sensor alert — our nearest mobile response unit dispatches within minutes. We maintain dedicated patrol fleets in Colombo, Gampaha, Kandy, Galle, Matara, and Jaffna for guaranteed fast deployment.

🤖
Reason 03

Technology-Integrated Security

We combine human expertise with AI-assisted CCTV analytics, digital guard tour systems (with GPS verification), and real-time incident reporting via our client portal. You get monthly security analytics reports and live dashboard access — so you always know exactly what's happening at your site.

🎓
Reason 04

Continuous Guard Training

Training doesn't stop at onboarding. Every Oracta officer undergoes 200+ hours of initial training plus quarterly refresher programs covering threat assessment, first aid, fire safety, conflict de-escalation, and customer interaction. Our Head of Training holds international security credentials from recognised global bodies.

🤝
Reason 05

Dedicated Account Management

Every client — from a single shopfront to a 50-site enterprise — is assigned a dedicated account manager. One person. Direct line. No call centres, no ticket queues, no runaround. Your manager conducts regular site reviews, handles all scheduling changes, and escalates issues to senior operations within 30 minutes.

🗺️
Reason 06

Scalable Island-Wide Coverage

Whether you need one officer at a single site or a coordinated team across 20 locations, Oracta scales seamlessly. Our operational presence across all 25 Sri Lankan districts means no matter where your business grows, your security coverage grows with it — with the same quality standards at every site.
