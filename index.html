Why Oracta

What Sets Us Apart

Every security company makes promises. Here's what makes ours different.

01

Licensed & Fully Compliant

All Oracta operations are licensed under Sri Lanka's Private Security Services Authority. Every guard is registered, background-verified, and legally deployed — giving you complete peace of mind and zero liability exposure.

02

Rapid Response Guarantee

Our control room operates 24/7. From the moment an incident is flagged, our nearest response unit is dispatched within minutes. We maintain a fleet of mobile patrol vehicles across all active districts for swift deployment.

03

Technology-Integrated Security

We combine human expertise with AI-assisted CCTV monitoring, digital guard tour systems, and real-time incident reporting. Clients receive monthly analytics reports and live dashboard access to their site's security status.

04

Continuous Guard Training

Training doesn't stop at onboarding. Every Oracta officer undergoes quarterly refresher programs covering threat assessment, first aid, fire safety, and customer interaction — ensuring consistent professional standards.

05

Dedicated Account Management

Every client is assigned a dedicated account manager — a single point of contact for scheduling, escalations, and strategic security reviews. No call centres. No runaround. Just direct access to someone who knows your site.

06

Scalable Island-Wide Coverage

Whether you need one guard at a single site or a coordinated team across 20 locations island-wide, Oracta scales with you. Our district presence across all 25 provinces ensures consistent service no matter where you operate.
